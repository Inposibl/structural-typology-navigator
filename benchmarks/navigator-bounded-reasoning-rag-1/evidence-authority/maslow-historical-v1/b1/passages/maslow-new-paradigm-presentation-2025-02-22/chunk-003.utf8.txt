Subject-Object (Resource-Oriented) Activity of Consciousness. The primary mode of consciousness activity, capable of intentionally objectifying things from the external world to quickly organize the process of mono-subjective activity. S-O activity can also be referred to as resource-oriented consciousness activity, aimed at seeking and possessing various types of resource-objects.

## Слайд 5. S–O — субъект-объектная / ресурсная активность

Mono-subjective activity of consciousness. The influence of external content is minimized in consciousness. Control is governed by physiological reflexes, directing a person's attention toward a state of physical pleasure. Except for sleep time, this type of activity is very short-term and is most prominent during the realization of the organism's biological drives.
